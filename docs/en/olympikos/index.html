How to play

  1. 1

    Setup

    Five rings in a row with a sport under each, the bronze, silver and gold medals, and every player with a delegation: France, Argentina, USA, Kenya, Australia or China.

  2. 2

    The Coach chooses

    The Coach draws six clues and randomly picks a card from their delegation: the ring color shows which sport to make the others guess. One clue goes face down under each medal, upright or sideways.

  3. 3

    Ready, set, vote!

    The Coach flips the three clues. Each Athlete places a Delegation card on each medal, on top of the last one. Once placed, a card can’t move.

  4. 4

    Medal ceremony

    Each stack is flipped: the first card is the Coach’s and gives the answer. The first Athlete who got it right scores 2, 3 or 4 points; the next ones score 1.

  5. 5

    Coach’s points

    If everyone got it, the Coach scores the medal’s points; if only some did, 1 point. If no one guessed any sport all round, the Coach loses 3.

  6. 6

    End of the game

    Everyone is Coach once. The player with the most points is the new Olympic champion.

The rules that make it special

Anti-doping control

From round two, the leading Athlete places one of their spare cards in front of the control: the sport they think wasn’t chosen. If they’re wrong, they lose 3 points.

Two players, against the clock

In co-op mode, a rival delegation lands on every medal every 10 seconds. Score together and check the international medal table: 36 points is a world record.

The Clue cards

71 objects to guess from

A whistle, a sail, a banana… The Coach picks the one that brings the sport to mind and stands it upright if it is a very good clue.
